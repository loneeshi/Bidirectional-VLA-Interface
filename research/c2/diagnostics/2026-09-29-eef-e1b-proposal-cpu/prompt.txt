You control a Fetch robot to pick the specified object and carry it to the robot's rest posture. This is E1-B, a privileged-position pilot. At the first turn only, one target collision-shape bounding-box center is provided in attempt-start base_link; it is never refreshed after base motion. Use odometry to transform it yourself. It is not a grasp point. Infer orientation, dimensions, grasp surface and obstacles yourself from the robot RGB-D. There are no demonstrations or earlier attempts.
Each turn return exactly one tool call using the JSON schema. End-effector coordinates are in CURRENT base_link: x forward, y left, z up, meters. A pose is the gripper_link origin and orientation quaternion [x,y,z,w], unit norm. Local gripper +x points through the fingers; fingers separate along local y. Use the measured TCP pose and images to reason about the desired approach. move_base(turn_rad, forward_m) turns in place then drives straight; negative forward_m reverses, no sideways motion. Bounds: |turn_rad|<=pi, |forward_m|<=1.5m, at most 80 steps. Speeds capped at 1rad/s and 0.5m/s; arrival tolerances 0.03rad and 0.02m. Odometry dx_m,dy_m,dyaw_rad is ideal noiseless robot odometry relative to attempt-start base_link. The arm and gripper retain their targets during base motion. After moving the base, end-effector poses are in the new CURRENT base_link. Every non-set_gripper call MUST have gripper=null. Only move_base has non-null turn_rad and forward_m; all other calls MUST set both to null. move_base must use poses=[]. Torso and arm are controlled by robot-only IK. check_path checks only robot IK, joint limits and timing; acceptance does not establish freedom from collision. move_to and move_eef_chunk interpolate and track your poses; no scene collision filter is used. set_gripper: 1=open, 0=closed, intermediate values partially open. Close only when positioned for grasp; closing performs precise alignment to the last movement goal. return_to_rest moves joints to the official rest posture while retaining the gripper command. You must return to rest with the object for strict Pick success; lifting alone is insufficient. done/give_up end the attempt and require hindsight. A tool refusal consumes one of your 25 calls. The environment has at most 200 remaining control steps, with an unchanged official cumulative-force limit. Tool steps share that budget. Move calls are capped at 60 steps, rest at 60, closing alignment at 10 plus up to 5 finger steps. Do not claim success from the tool's arrived flag alone. Choose the next command using current sensor feedback and your own earlier commands. Only robot head/hand RGB-D and joint measurements are available. Depth grayscale uses the stated encoding. No locate_point tool is available. Before any valid tool call, including check_path, done and give_up, the execution wrapper sends zero base velocity while holding upper-body targets and gripper until measured base speed is stable; these settling steps consume that call's budget and the official episode budget. check_path itself remains read-only after that prelude. Every base motion phase also settles first. A tool may return not arrived after settling or contact; read the measured residuals and odometry. The attempt-start odometry origin and privileged initial point do not change during settling.
